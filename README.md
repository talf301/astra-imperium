# Astra Imperium

An unofficial, rules-enforced phone version of *Astra Imperium*, the solo resource management game by Raphael Rochedix (development Joe Klipfel, art Cole Munro-Chitty, graphic design Drew Corkill; Best with 1 Games). It is a static web app built for iPhone: open it in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen, and it runs full screen and offline.

The app is built from the print-and-play files ("Astra Imperium Rules Final PnP" and the Letter print sheets). Every card, tile, mat and scenario card shown is the original art; the numbers were read from the printed faces.

## What is implemented

- The standard game on the three standard map mats (Earth 2255, Galaxia Nova (A), Constantius (A)).
- The 21 base Action cards and the Edict of Kallax Calla.
- Modular monuments: all 18 tiles (basic, variant I, variant II of the 6 monuments), chosen, random, or basic.
- The 6 leaders.
- Campaign 1, Rise of the Empire (9 scenario cards) and Campaign 2, Fall of the Empire (7 scenario cards), with the three difficulty levels and the branching from the card backs.
- Saving after every decision, and undo back to the last point where a card was revealed.

Not implemented: Campaign 3 (A Desperate Journey), Campaign 4 (Constantius), Campaign 5 (War of All Wars), the 6 modular invader mats, and Epic Campaign mode. Campaigns 3 to 5 each replace the map and the invasions with their own sub-game (planet travel and probe flicking, tribe tiles and a locked deck, six invader mats with their own rules), and Epic mode needs all five campaigns. Their cards are in the print sheets, so they can be added.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8477 --bind 127.0.0.1        # from the repository root, then open http://127.0.0.1:8477/public/
node --test tests/*.test.mjs                         # rules cases, random-play fuzzing, greedy bot
FUZZ_SEEDS=5000 node --test tests/fuzz.test.mjs      # more random games
python3 -I tools/build_sw.py public astra-imperium   # after every change under public/: new version stamp
```

`tools/frame.html` shows the app in an iPhone-sized frame (`?h=660` sets the height); `tools/shot.sh out.png 'query'` screenshots it, and `tools/montage.sh out.png 660 'name=query' ...` puts several side by side. Development query parameters: `?new={setup}` starts a game (`{"leader":"kallax","scenario":"1.2e","tiles":{"nova":2}}`), `&seed=N`, `&auto=N&bot=greedy|random` plays N decisions, `&until=invasion` then plays on to that kind of decision, `&sel=0` selects a hand card, `&sheet=menu` opens a sheet, `&click=selector;selector` taps elements in turn. `?screen=setup|campaign&camp=c1&campstate={...}&prefs={...}` opens the other screens.

To rebuild the images from the print-and-play PDF (needs poppler's `pdftoppm`):

```sh
uv run --no-project --with pillow python -I tools/build_assets.py \
  "$HOME/Downloads/Astra Imperium PnP/Letter/Letter-NoBleed-AstraImperium.pdf" public/img /tmp/astra-sheets
```

The optional third argument writes contact sheets for checking the crops.

## How it works

- `public/engine/data.mjs` holds the cards, tiles, maps, leaders and scenario cards as compact tables.
- `public/engine/game.mjs` is the rules engine. State is plain JSON and all randomness comes from a seeded generator in the state. The game is a queue of steps; a step that needs the player leaves a decision in `state.ask` (`{kind, text, options}`) and `act(state, optionId)` returns the next state. `payOptions` and `gainOptions` list every distinct way to pay a cost or take a gain, so Novastage's interchangeable resources and "resources of your choice" are ordinary decisions.
- `public/app.mjs` draws `state.ask` and answers it. It computes no rules.
- `tests/helpers.mjs` holds the random and greedy bots; the app imports them for the development parameters.
- `public/sw.js` and `public/version.mjs` are generated by `tools/build_sw.py`; the version is a hash of every file and is shown on the title screen and in the menu.

## Rulings

Where the rulebook is silent or ambiguous the app does this:

- **Edition.** Rules follow "Astra Imperium Rules Final PnP". The map is named "Earth 2255" on the mat and "Earth 2250" once in the rulebook; the app uses the mat.
- **Resource track.** Resources stop at 9 (the Resource Board has no higher space); the excess is lost.
- **Structure bonus.** "+2 every time you gain" applies once per gain: once for a card played for Resources (however many icons), once for a tribute, once for a conquered location. The bonus is given for the resource you actually take.
- **Novastage (basic, II).** Each unit you gain or spend of one of the two resources may be the other instead, unit by unit, for every gain and every cost, invasions included. The app lists the distinct results and you pick one.
- **Novastage I.** The second card is mandatory when you have another face-up card, and both cards count as one gain for structure bonuses.
- **Completed monuments.** A monument holds 2 cubes. Once it is complete its cards can only be played for Resources.
- **RSS Mars 1 (basic).** Invasions are ignored automatically; you cannot choose to pay instead.
- **RSS Mars 1 variant I.** The whole invasion cost is paid in a single resource type of your choice.
- **RSS Mars 1 variant II.** The tile shows 1 VP (the rulebook text omits it). The free conquest also gives the location's resource, and Starjan's surcharge does not apply to it.
- **Orbit Urbis II.** "The value of each built Structure" is its printed VP (Solar Harvester and Holo-Muse: 2; the others: 0).
- **Quantum Barterdeck I.** One resource type of your choice is counted; the app takes the best one.
- **Data Vault II.** Adjacent on the Monument Board are Quantum Barterdeck (above), Orbit Urbis (beside) and RSS Mars 1 (below).
- **Orbital Fluxway (basic).** Counts 1 VP per location; leader or scenario changes to location VP do not change it.
- **Invasions.** You may choose to lose locations even when you could pay. You choose which locations to lose; Rome is lost only when you have no other location left. There is no partial payment.
- **Edict of Kallax Calla.** Pay 1 Military, then take the Action of a card in the discard pile, paying that card's cost as well. Copying a structure builds that card out of the discard pile. Scenario effects that trigger "when you play" a card do not trigger for a copy.
- **Cyberius, From Capri.** The face-down card cannot be played until you pay 1 resource of any type to flip it. Treason Trials adds 1 of one resource type shown on the card.
- **Commodorus, Hercules.** Checked before you draw, and not when the deck is empty (the round ends instead of a turn starting). With Plague the five structure cards start in the discard pile, so the first round has 16 cards.
- **Calculigula, Wasted Resources.** Applies after the first and second rounds' invasions even when RSS Mars 1 ignored them.
- **Leader and scenario together.** The scenario wins a conflict: Voidspasian in I.II Dictator starts with 2 of each resource, as the scenario says. Commodorus in I.III Tyrant keeps the Mining Colony the scenario gives.
- **I.III Emperor.** Only an invasion paid for in Military counts as repelled (Science spent as Military through Novastage counts). An invasion ignored by RSS Mars 1, or paid in another resource through its variant I, does not.
- **I.IV Tyrant.** Choosing or being forced to lose a location loses the game at once.
- **II.I Crisis.** The 3 starting locations are in addition to Rome and give no resources. Each of the two invasions is a separate choice.
- **II.II Ludem et Circenses.** The cube option is offered whenever you hold an RSS Mars 1 card and can pay 3 resources, even when the monument is complete.
- **II.II Inflation.** The payment comes before each invasion and cannot be skipped.
- **II.III Plunder.** The cube is removed first; RSS Mars 1 then ignores the invasion only if it is still complete.
- **II.IV Decay.** Applies to completed monuments and can make one worth less than 0.
- **II.IV Corruption.** Offered when you play a Call for Tribute card for its Action; one cube per play.
- **A game lost** (Rome falls, or a scenario's losing condition) is a defeat for the scenario; the campaign continues on the defeat branch.
