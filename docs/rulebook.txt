INTRODUCTION
There were close calls, but the Roman Empire en-
dured. Guided by the honor and traditions of the
Ancients, Rome survived long enough for you to shape
its future. No longer will the Eternal Empire settle for
regional power. Under your rule, Rome will rise to
global dominance, and then, who knows—even the sky
may pose no limit.

Manage the Empire’s resources, strengthen its
economy, lead armies to glorious conquest, and
construct magnificent monuments to honor your
ancestors. But beware—enemies gather at your
borders, waiting for a chance to strike. Can you lead
Rome to greatness while preserving the legacy of the
Ancients?

OVERVIEW
Astra Imperium is a fast-playing resource
management game for one. Each turn, you’ll draw
cards and make tough decisions about how to
use them. Gather industry, science, and military
resources to conquer locations, construct powerful
structures, and build monuments that honor the
Ancients and earn Veneration Points (VP). But
stay prepared—enemy invasions grow stronger
each round, and Rome must be defended for 3
rounds before you can total your final score and
determine how well you played.

Beyond standalone games, Astra Imperium also
features 5 unique campaigns that dramatically
change gameplay and encourage new strategies.
Lead Rome through its rise to power, periods
of decline, interstellar expansion, planetary
settlement, and more.

Play campaigns individually, or tackle them
in sequence and track your Reputation in Epic
Campaign mode.

Each game of Astra Imperium is played over 3
rounds. Each round consists of several turns, and
each turn you must follow these 3 steps:
    1. Draw 3 cards
    2. Play 1 card for either its Resources or Action
    3. Discard all drawn cards

          Act I: Pay 2  OR remove 1 Control Cube

After resolving the third round’s invasion, the game

EPIC CAMPAIGNS
In Epic Campaign mode, you will play through
all 5 campaigns in order from 1 to 5. After each
campaign, you will record a running Reputation
score, and the goal is to have as high of a
Reputation as possible at the end of all campaigns.
You start an Epic Campaign with a Reputation
Score of 0. Your Reputation will go up or down
after each campaign, and can end as either positive
or negative.
Each time you complete the final scenario of a
campaign, use the table below to determine the
change to your Reputation score:

     Overwhelming Victory               +4
                  Victory               +2
           Narrow Victory               +1
            Narrow Defeat               -1
                   Defeat               -2
      Overwhelming Defeat               -3

There were many who doubted, but the Roman
Empire persisted through the millennia. Now, we set
our sights on something greater: global dominance.

Campaign 2: Fall of the Empire
We achieved our goal of becoming a global empire, but
at great cost. We stretched ourselves thin. Power and

though preserved for millennia, begins to crumble.
“Fall of the Empire” takes you through a challenging
series of scenarios. Crises like poverty, inflation, decay

each stage of the Fall campaign, VP requirements get
easier, but circumstances get harder as you deal with

Special Action Cards
TAKE OFF cards - When played for its Action, these
cards allow you to move your civilization ship to
the next planet following the dotted path. To do so,
you must first pay the cost indicated in the box on
the dotted line you are about to travel along, then
you move your ship to the next planet, gaining the
resource printed on the planet.
When traveling to the final planet, Constantius,
you do not have to pay any cost and you don’t
receive any resource after making the trip, but this
path can only be travelled during the 4th and final
Scenario of the campaign.
At the end of each Scenario, you gain the indicated
number of VP next to the final planet you ended on.
Launch Probes cards - When played for its Action,
these cards allow you to attempt to launch a probe
toward Constantius to better understand your

future homeworld.
To do so, first pay 2  per probe you have already
landed on Constantius this Scenario (therefore the
first probe has no added cost).
Then place a Probe disc on the planet on which
your civilization ship currently sits and propel
it toward Constantius with a flick of your index
finger. If the Probe disc ends its travel so that
ANY part of the Probe disc is in contact with
Constantius, the launch is successful and the Probe
disc is then placed on the current Scenario card.
If your Probe token does not land on Constantius
but ends near it, you can spend a resource of your
choice for a trajectory correction, placing a Control
cube touching the Probe disc in the direction
of Constantius. Continue taking trajectory
corrections, paying a resource and placing a cube
until the cube you just placed comes in contact
with Constantius. The launch is then considered
successful and the Probe disc is placed on the
current Scenario card.
Note, trajectory corrections are optional. A Probe
launch can also end in a failure, though you must
still pay the initial cost to launch the probe.
Each successfully launched Probe, represented by
a Probe disc on the Scenario card, provides either 1
or 2 VP (see that scenario’s card).

Scenario Completion
The first 3 Scenario cards for this campaign list 3
different VP goals for each difficulty level, referred
to as the “Low VP”, “Middle VP” and “High VP”
values. To complete the Scenario successfully,
you must earn at least the “Low VP” value shown

earn this level of VP, you lose the campaign in an
Overwhelming Defeat. Additionally, each Scenario
has a printed goal that must be completed in order
to successfully complete the Scenario.
If you earned a number of VP equal to or exceeding
the “Low VP” , you have successfully completed
the Scenario. Compare your final VP value to these
three printed values for your chosen difficulty

your achieved level is right-side up and place the
card near the Scenario deck to remember this

Campaign 4: CONSTANTIUS
The Empire’s fleet reached Constantius, the distant
planet identified by our scientists as our best hope
for a new home. Not long after touching down,
we made two despair-inducing realizations. First,
this world and its landscapes are less familiar and
more unforgiving than we anticipated. The second
discovery is even more unsettling—we are not alone
on this planet. A sophisticated alien society known
as the Trusks is initially hostile to our presence.
Normally, war would be Rome’s solution. But in
our journey of desperation across the stars, we’ve
turned over a new leaf. Your mandate as Emperor is
to somehow engineer peace and cooperation between
Rome and the various Trusk factions. You have 4

Special Action Card Rules
ADAPT AND LEARN cards - You can use these
cards’ actions to unlock additional cards from your
locked deck. To do so, pay 10 minus the number
of cards still in the locked deck (so the first time
you take this action during Scenario I, it will cost
(10-9) = 1 ). Then draw a random card from the
locked deck and add it to your discard pile.
CONQUEST cards - When taking the Conquest
action, you pay the usual cost of 1  for each
Control cube currently on the map. After paying
the cost, you may place a new Control cube on any
space adjacent to one of your Control cubes already
on the map. Flip any adjacent Tribe tile that is not
already flipped.
The Tribe tiles corresponding to spaces in which
you have Control cubes will provide optional
bonuses at the end of the round and have no
immediate effect.

instead of the usual 3. Your draw deck starts out
with only 14 cards in the first Scenario but can
grow as each Scenario continues.
At the end of each of the 4 rounds, you have the

active Tribe tiles (a Tribe tile is active when you
have a control cube on the map in the space linked
to that Tribe tile). After round 1, you can gain all
bonuses without paying a

Tribe Bonuses card for the

all Tribe bonuses. The front

show the costs for rounds 3
and 4. These Tribe Bonuses
are described in more detail
on the next page.

Campaign 5: War of All Wars
The Empire’s new posture of peace is short-lived.
It turns out that we were not the only nation to
escape the dying Earth. Your old rivals have landed
on Constantius and they are not interested in
cooperation. They attack the Empire’s cities and
outposts with a ferocity not known since the days of
the Ancients. The Aegis Hellenarcy. The Dawnward
Crown. The Gallax Hordes. Even the Alpsbreaker
Armada. All are converging on Rome, and it’s your
job as Emperor to save your people. In each stage of
the War of all Wars campaign, you must face a new
invading force with its own unique mechanics. Can
you navigate this treacherous war and maintain
the legacy of Rome? And if you succeed at that, can
you finally extend the Empire’s reach across the
galaxy, fulfilling Rome’s destiny to become the Astra
Imperium: the Empire of the stars?

Gameplay
Each Scenario of this campaign is played as a
standard game with only the changes to the rules
written on the Modular Invader card and the
current Scenario card.
At the end of each Scenario, total your score (with
any adjustments from the Modular Invader card)
and compare it to the VP requirement for your

                                                   33
chosen difficulty on the Scenario card. If you met
this VP value AND fulfilled any extra win conditions
on the Scenario card and/or the Modular Invader
card, you have won the Scenario and may flip the
Scenario card to read the Victory text, continuing/
ending the campaign as usual.
If you did not meet the VP requirement or failed

the Scenario and/or Modular Invader card, flip the
Scenario card to read the Defeat text, continuing/
